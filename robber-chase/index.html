🚓 Robber Chase

Catch the robber with more loot!

For grown-ups: ask “Which robber has more loot?” and let your child tap that robber. If they pick the other one, it gets away, and the two piles line up coin by coin so the extra coins stand out. Count the coins out loud together as they fly into the chest.
