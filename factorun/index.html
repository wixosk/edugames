🐙 Kraken Crossing

Steer your boat to the right number and the kraken lets you through.

Timer off: the kraken waits until you press GO. Timer on: the boat speeds up when answers are quick and correct, and slows down after mistakes.
Facts your child gets right quickly 3 times in a row lose their dot pictures. A mistake brings the pictures back.
