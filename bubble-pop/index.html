🫧 Bubble Pop

Tap two bubbles that make the number on the sign. Pop as many as you can!

For grown-ups: each round lasts one minute. Tap one bubble, then its partner: 3 and 7 make 10. After the first tap the sign shows “3 + ? = 10” and a ten-frame with the gaps to fill. A wrong pair just bounces apart and shows what it does make. Wait a moment and the right partner starts to glow.
